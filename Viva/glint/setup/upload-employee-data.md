# Prepare and upload your employee data to Viva Glint

Microsoft Viva Glint refers to employee data as "attributes." Attributes are data about the people in your organization, which are then used to convert feedback into insightful and action-oriented intelligence to improve employee engagement and achieve business goals.

## Meet with internal stakeholders

Ensure that all users can filter to the right insights by meeting with the right team of people to decide which attributes to include in your Glint data. Some potential stakeholders are:

- HR information system (HRIS) vendors
- IT and security team members
- HR business analysts
- Your employee engagement team
- Executive leadership 

## Sample data preparation timeline

|Timeframe  |Roles involved   |Activity|
|:----------|:-----------|:------------|
| Week 1     |   <ul><li>Viva Glint admin</li><li>IT and security teams</li></ul>       | <ul><li>Review data [requirements](data-checklist.md) and [best practices](attribute-fundamentals.md)</li><li>Set a meeting with internal stakeholders</li><li>Contact IT and security teams for [SFTP connection](set-up-sftp.md) and [allowlist](allowed-list.md) updates (if needed)</li></ul>        |
| Week 2     |<ul><li>Viva Glint admin</li><li>HRIS vendor</li><li>IT and security teams</li><li>HR business analysts</li></ul>        |<ul><li>Meet with stakeholders</li><li>Finalize file layout and format with the [Attribute Template](create-employee-attribute-template.md)</li><li>Select a [file upload method](choose-upload-method.md)</li></ul>         |
| Week 3     | <ul><li>Viva Glint admin</li><li>HRIS vendor</li></ul>       | <ul><li>Prepare an HRIS file</li><li>[Review data file](data-checklist.md) for alignment with requirements</li><li>[Set up attributes](send-employee-attributes.md) in Glint</li><li>Upload data in Glint</li><li>[Troubleshoot](/viva/troubleshoot/glint/data-file-upload/data-file-upload-warnings-errors) issues</li></ul>          |
| Ongoing    | <ul><li>Viva Glint admin</li><li>HRIS vendor</li></ul>       | <ul><li>Data quality checks for accuracy/consistency</li><li>Regular file uploads to keep employee data current</li><li>[Update attributes](update-attributes.md) in Glint for new data</li></ul>         |
