# Manager Quick Guide - Results and Conversations

It's not uncommon for managers to struggle to use survey results as tools to have quality conversations with their teams. Whether they have years of experience, or are a new manager seeing Microsoft Viva Glint results for the first time, it's an exciting - but sometimes unnerving - experience. With so much data available, it can be easy to fall into negative behaviors and thought patterns when interpreting results.

The Viva People Science research team identified common pitfalls that managers might encounter when owning their own survey results. Equip them with knowledge to combat these pitfalls. Let them know that *pitfalls do exist* and then guide them on how to avoid them.

The role of manager is central to the success of a continued conversation approach to having happier employees and improving business. Commit to your employee’s feedback survey strategy:

- Become familiar with the Viva Glint platform and interpreting feedback results.
- Discuss survey results with your teams and peers.
- Take small, visible, data-driven action.
- Have ongoing conversations with your team about progress.
